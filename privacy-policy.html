Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Information We Collect

The Morse Code Audio & Signal Trainer is designed to minimize data collection and protect your privacy. Here's what you should know:

No Personal Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information such as:

  • Your name or identity
  • Email addresses or contact information
  • Location data
  • Account credentials
  • Payment information

Local Storage

We use your browser's localStorage functionality to save certain settings and practice data on your device. This includes:

  • Practice scores and statistics
  • Audio settings (WPM, tone frequency, volume)
  • Practice preferences and difficulty settings

This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to any server. It is used solely to enhance your experience by remembering your preferences and progress between sessions.

How We Use Information

Local Storage Data

Data stored in localStorage is used to:

  • Remember your audio settings between visits
  • Track your practice progress and scores
  • Maintain your preferred practice modes and difficulty levels
  • Provide a consistent user experience

No Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or distribute any data with third parties. Since we don't collect personal data or transmit localStorage data to servers, there is no data to share.

Cookies and Tracking

No Tracking Cookies

We do not use tracking cookies or similar tracking technologies. We do not track your browsing behavior or analyze your usage patterns for marketing purposes.

Essential Local Storage

The localStorage we use is essential for the functionality of the practice tools (saving settings and progress). This is not a cookie and is not used for tracking.

Third-Party Services

We do not use third-party services that collect personal data, such as:

  • Analytics services
  • Advertising networks
  • Social media integration
  • Third-party authentication

The website operates as a standalone static application without external service dependencies that would require data sharing.

Data Security

Local Storage Security

Since localStorage data remains on your device, its security depends on your device's security practices:

  • Data is stored in your browser and accessible only to websites in the same domain
  • Clearing your browser data will remove localStorage data
  • Using private/incognito browsing mode prevents localStorage persistence
  • Anyone with access to your device may access localStorage data

No Server-Side Storage

We do not store any data on servers. This eliminates risks associated with server breaches, data leaks, or unauthorized server access.

Your Rights and Choices

Controlling Local Storage

You have control over localStorage data:

  • Clear Data: You can clear localStorage at any time through your browser settings
  • Private Browsing: Use private/incognito mode to prevent localStorage persistence
  • Browser Settings: Configure your browser to block or clear site data
  • Reset Progress: Use the "Reset Score" buttons in our practice tools to clear progress data

No Account Required

Since we don't require accounts, there's no personal data to delete, update, or transfer. All data is stored locally on your device.

Children's Privacy

Our website is suitable for users of all ages, including children. Since we do not collect personal information, we do not knowingly collect data from children under any age. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of the internet and browser settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Significant changes to our data practices would be prominently noted on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or our data practices, please review our Terms of Use or FAQ section for additional information.
