Number Practice Tips

Pattern Recognition

Morse code numbers follow a clear pattern: 0 is five dashes, 5 is five dots, and the numbers in between mix dots and dashes. The count of dots increases from 0 to 5, then decreases back to 0.

Start Slow

Numbers can be tricky because they're longer than most letters. Start at 5-10 WPM to ensure you can hear each element clearly before increasing speed.

Practice in Context

Numbers often appear in coordinates, frequencies, and measurements. After learning individual numbers, try the mixed practice mode to practice numbers alongside letters.

Common Uses

Numbers are essential for:

  • Amateur radio frequencies (e.g., 7.025 MHz)
  • Coordinates and positions
  • Signal reports (e.g., 59 signal report)
  • Time and dates
  • Measurements and quantities
