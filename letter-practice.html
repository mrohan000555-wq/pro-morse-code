Letter Practice Tips

Beginner Mode

Focus on the 12 most common letters first. These letters appear most frequently in English text, so learning them gives you the most practical benefit. Start at 5-10 WPM and increase speed as you improve.

Intermediate Mode

Practice all 26 letters of the alphabet. This mode helps you learn less common letters and ensures you can recognize any letter when it appears in communication.

Challenge Mode

Random letters at higher speeds. This mode tests your ability to recognize letters quickly and is great for building speed and fluency. Recommended for experienced learners.

Common Letter Groupings

Learning letters in groups can help with recognition:

Short Letters (1-2 elements)

E (.), T (-), I (..), A (.-), N (-.), M (--)

Medium Letters (3-4 elements)

S (...), U (..-), R (.-.), W (.--), D (-..), K (-.-), G (--.), O (---)

Longer Letters (5+ elements)

H (....), B (-...), V (...-), F (..-.), L (.-..), P (.--.), J (.---), Q (--.-), Y (-.--), Z (--..)
