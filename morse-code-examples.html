Practice Tips

Using Examples for Learning

These examples are great for:

  • Pattern Recognition: Listen to common words to recognize familiar patterns
  • Speed Building: Practice phrases at increasing speeds
  • Context Learning: Understand how characters flow together in words
  • Real-World Prep: Practice with actual communication phrases

Recommended Practice

  1. Start with single words at slow speed (5-10 WPM)
  2. Progress to short phrases as you improve
  3. Try the custom player with your own practice text
  4. Gradually increase speed as your recognition improves

Timing and Rhythm

Notice how the rhythm changes between short words (like "YES") and longer words (like "MORNING"). The spacing between letters and words is just as important as the dots and dashes themselves.
